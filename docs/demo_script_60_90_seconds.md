# 60–90 Second Demonstration Script

A KPI can keep the same name while a definition change reverses the decision it supports.

We hold the source records and decision contract fixed. Version one divides activated accounts by activity-qualified accounts; version two divides them by all eligible matured signups. For May Alpha, the numerator stays seven while the denominator grows from eight to ten. The KPI falls from 87.5 to 70 percent, launch status flips from pass to fail, and Alpha drops behind Beta.

For history, splicing May under version one to June under version two suggests a 7.5-point decline, but that comparison is prohibited. Restating both months under version two shows a valid 10-point improvement.

April cannot be restated because the all-signup population was not retained. The plan is to restate and dual-report May through June, start the official version-two series in June, and mark April and cross-version stitches non-comparable. Approval remains pending.

If the source context changes too, the trial refuses definition-only attribution and returns `REJECT_PENDING_EVIDENCE`.

This is not Goodhart: behavior stays fixed while the metric’s meaning changes.
