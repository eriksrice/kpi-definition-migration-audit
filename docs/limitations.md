# Limitations

- The repository demonstrates one synthetic activation-rate definition migration. It does not prove cross-domain generality.
- Source coverage and provenance are declared inputs. The evaluator does not discover warehouse history or validate external storage systems.
- The 80% threshold and two-cohort bridge are synthetic scenario assumptions, not empirically optimized business policies.
- A `PARTIAL_RESTATEMENT` result is a policy-derived recommendation. Human approval remains `PENDING`.
- The controlled-context refusal demonstrates a narrow fail-closed attribution rule, not a general causal-inference system.
- Exact local replay and byte-stable artifacts do not establish production readiness, enterprise scale, operational security, or performance.
- The project has no dashboard, database, warehouse connector, deployment layer, LLM, agent, or organization-wide approval workflow.
- The fixtures contain synthetic records only and make no claim about real customers or protected data.
